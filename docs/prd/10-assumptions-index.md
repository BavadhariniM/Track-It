# 10. Assumptions Index

- FR-8 — "Workdays" is a user-configurable preset (not hardcoded Monday–Friday).
- FR-25 — Goals can be organized into categories, and category filtering is in MVP (not just a data-model placeholder).
- FR-30 — Reminders are suppressed once a Goal's current-period target is already met.
- FR-32 — Widget tap-through opens the app to the relevant date/Goal, subject to platform capability.
